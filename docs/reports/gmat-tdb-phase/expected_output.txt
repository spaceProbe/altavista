Where the phase shift comes from: passing refJd=0.0 to TimeSystemConverter::Convert()
(the default is GmatTimeConstants::JD_JAN_5_1941 = 2,430,000.0) leaves the periodic
term's T_TT computed from an origValue still on GMAT's internal MJD scale, short by
exactly the 2430000-day offset derived above -- reproducing the GMAT-phase series
to sub-microsecond precision, and reproducing GMAT's own Sat.TDBModJulian nowhere.

NOT A GMAT DEFECT: Sat.TDBModJulian - Sat.TTModJulian matches the correct (undisplaced) series at every epoch and disagrees with the GMAT-phase series by 1.629e-03 s or more; the GMAT-phase series is reproduced only by passing refJd=0.0 explicitly, which is not TimeSystemConverter::Convert()'s own default and not what any call site read in GMAT's own R2026a source passes.
